01 · Marketing strategy

Chef Jen Cuisine

Objective: Develop a 90-day plan to increase brand awareness, website traffic, social engagement, and online orders.

Approach: Identified audience groups and brand positioning, then proposed Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook content built around product education, recipe demonstrations, social proof, user-generated content, and promotions.

Deliverables: Proposed campaign goals, platform strategy, content themes, weekly schedule, and measurement ideas.

Skills demonstrated: Marketing planning, audience thinking, content strategy, and KPI selection.

Academic project. Goals and growth figures in the original plan are proposed targets, not measured campaign results.
Campaign strategy & planned activations

How the plan aimed to build engagement and encourage purchases

The original 90-day plan focused on making the seasoning brand useful, visible, and easy to buy from through consistent social content and clear calls to action.

01

Recipe demonstrations

Short-form cooking videos showing how to use the seasoning in everyday meals. The goal: make the product tangible and give audiences a reason to save, share, and try recipes.

02

Product education

Posts explaining flavor, product uses, and ways to incorporate the seasoning into different dishes—helping potential customers understand what makes the product useful.

03

User-generated content

A proposed community challenge inviting customers to share meals made with the seasoning. Reposts and a branded hashtag would help encourage participation and broaden reach.

04

Social proof

Feature customer feedback, testimonials, and user-submitted food photos to build trust and give prospective buyers examples of how others use the product.

05

Promotions & bundles

Use limited-time offers, product bundles, and gift-oriented promotions to give interested shoppers a clear reason to visit the website and place an order.

06

Brand storytelling

Share the brand story and its flavor inspiration to create a more recognizable identity and a connection beyond the product itself.

These are strategy elements and a sample content rhythm based on the original academic plan. The posts, schedule, and measurement framework are proposed examples—not evidence of a campaign that ran or results that were achieved.
02 · Sports marketing

Charlotte Hornets Game Experience

Objective: Examine persuasion techniques used to influence the live game-day consumer experience.

Approach: Observed examples including scarcity, anticipation, social proof, nostalgia, merchandise pricing, player endorsements, giveaways, contests, concessions, and promotional bundles.

Deliverable: An academic analysis connecting in-arena observations to persuasion and consumer behavior concepts.

Skills demonstrated: Observation, sports marketing analysis, audience engagement, and written communication.

Academic observational project; not commissioned by or affiliated with the Charlotte Hornets.
03 · Brand experience

Miami Heat Fan Experience Audit

Objective: Evaluate elements of the fan experience at a Miami Heat home game.

Approach: Conducted an observational audit and organized findings into a SWOT analysis covering arena entertainment, atmosphere, technology, pricing, engagement, access, personalization, and competition.

Deliverable: Fan-experience audit with recommendations related to pricing, engagement, accessibility, and safety.

Skills demonstrated: Research, SWOT analysis, customer experience evaluation, and strategic recommendations.

Academic observational project; not commissioned by or affiliated with the Miami Heat.
